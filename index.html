About Me

I am a highly skilled Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) with around 9 years of comprehensive experience in software automation and manual testing. My career has been dedicated to ensuring the quality, reliability, and performance of complex enterprise applications.

I possess deep expertise in building scalable test automation frameworks using cutting-edge tools like Selenium WebDriver (Java) and Playwright (TypeScript/JavaScript), alongside robust API testing with Rest Assured and WireMock. My background also includes strong proficiency in core Java, OOP principles, and integrating testing into CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI/CD, Jenkins).

I am adept at various testing methodologies, including functional, regression, integration, and performance testing (JMeter), with a keen eye for detail in defect tracking and reporting. My experience extends to Accessibility Testing (WCAG 2.1 AA) and working effectively within Agile Scrum and Kanban environments. I am passionate about leveraging automation to streamline development cycles and enhance product excellence.

I was honored with an Excellence award from OpenText for significantly reducing manual testing efforts, a testament to my commitment to efficiency and innovation.
